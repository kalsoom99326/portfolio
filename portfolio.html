    Try it: why the decision threshold matters

    A churn model gives each customer a score. You pick the cut-off above which you call them "will leave". Missing a real churner costs $600 and a false alarm costs $60, so the cheapest cut-off is far below 0.5. This demo uses simulated scores to show the idea, not my model's real output.
